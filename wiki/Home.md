Welcome to the carl-rpg wiki!

This is an unofficial Foundry VTT system for the _Dungeon Crawler Carl_ tabletop RPG. It's a fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Matt Dinniman or Renegade Game Studios.

The system doesn't ship any rulebook text or art. If you own the PDFs, you can import them into your world. See [Importing](Importing).

## Where to Start

- **New to the system?** Start with [Getting Started](Getting-Started).
- **Making a crawler?** See [Character Creation](Character-Creation).
- **Running a fight?** See [Combat](Combat).
- **Handing out loot?** See [Loot Boxes and Achievements](Loot-Boxes-and-Achievements).
- **Running the game?** The [GM Toolbox](GM-Toolbox) is where most GM actions live.
